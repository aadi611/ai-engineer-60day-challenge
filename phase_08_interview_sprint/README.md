# Phase 8: Interview Sprint (Days 58-60)

## Days

- **Day 58** — Resume/portfolio/LinkedIn polish, tailored to target AI Engineer roles. Draft STAR stories from the capstone and other phase projects (a shipped project + a specific tradeoff decision is a strong STAR answer).
- **Day 59** — Full mock interview loop: one technical round, one system design round, one behavioral round. Record yourself if possible and self-review.
- **Day 60** — Gap-fill on whatever the mocks exposed, final skim of your notes/cheat sheets across all phases, rest.

## Deliverable

A tailored resume, 5-6 STAR stories mapped to common behavioral questions, and a self-review from the mock loop identifying your top 2-3 remaining weak spots.
